Mandatory Rules & Guidelines (2027):

• Dress Code: Formal Dress or College Uniform must be worn during the internship program. Non-formal dress is strictly not allowed for both Male & Female interns.

• Attendance & Logbook: 100% attendance is required without fail. Bring your logbook daily for signatures. If absent, the logbook will not be signed.

• Property & Conduct: Any damage to institution furniture will require full payment by the responsible intern. Zero tolerance for disrespect/arguing with faculty (certificate will be held).

• Strict Violations: Any violation by male or female interns will result in internship certificates being withheld. Fighting between interns will result in the institute directly handing over individuals to the police station.

• Prohibitions: Drinking, smoking, and gutkha/tobacco consumption are strictly prohibited.

• Devices: Bringing your own laptop or tablet is strongly recommended for better experience (smartphone is acceptable if unavailable).
